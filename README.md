# Gamify Life

A personal life tracker that turns self-improvement into an RPG. Track your
progress across five pillars, check off quests to earn XP, level up, and get
suggestions for new things to try.

## Pillars

- **Knowledge** - learn new skills, read books, and practice your craft
- **Speech** - practice friendliness, greetings, and communicating with different people
- **Health** - build healthy habits and track/reduce harmful ones (sugar, nicotine, THC, caffeine, gaming time)
- **Purpose** - find fulfillment, plan vacations and life events, and live with intention
- **Finance** - build job, marketing, and investing skills, and grow your wealth

## Features

- Each pillar tracks its own XP and level, plus an overall level
- A starter list of quests per pillar, with daily (repeatable) and one-time quests
- A suggestions list you can add to your active quest list at any time
- A custom quest form to add your own goals with a chosen XP value
- A Health habit tracker for daily logging of harmful habits, with goals and streaks
- All progress is saved locally in your browser (localStorage)
